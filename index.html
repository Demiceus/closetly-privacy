Closetly Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Overview

Closetly is a personal wardrobe management application that helps users organize clothing items and receive AI-powered outfit recommendations.

Information We Store

Closetly may store information that users enter into the application, such as clothing names, categories, colors, clothing images, and wardrobe-related information.

Local Storage

Wardrobe information and clothing images may be stored locally on the user's device for the application's functionality.

AI Services

Closetly may send wardrobe-related information to its AI service to generate outfit recommendations and answer wardrobe-related questions.

Pinterest

Closetly may use Pinterest's developer platform and API services to provide fashion-related information and inspiration. Pinterest data is used only for the functionality described within the application.

Data Sharing

Closetly does not sell users' personal information. Information is only sent to third-party services when necessary to provide application functionality.

Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by Closetly. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Closetly's features change. Updated versions will be published on this page.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or Closetly, please contact the developer through the contact information associated with the project.
